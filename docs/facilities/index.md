# Facilities

TokEye analyzes fluctuating signals as time series or spectrograms. These pages introduce facility contexts and how to use exported diagnostic data with TokEye.

- [DIII-D National Fusion Facility](diii-d.md): the tokamak where the diagnostics used to develop TokEye were measured.
- [Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory (PPPL)](pppl.md): a plasma research laboratory whose experiments include NSTX-U and collaborations at other facilities.

TokEye reads local files; it does not connect directly to a facility's data systems. Access and export data through your facility's own tools, then see [input formats](../guides/input-formats.md).
